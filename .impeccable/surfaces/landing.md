# Surface: landing page (`src/app/page.tsx`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: busy adults in Europe (see PRODUCT.md). Action: join the waitlist (email), with an optional follow-up question. Scope: landing page only; the signed-in app keeps its current look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Kalends is a station departures board for your life admin: bills, documents and contracts arrive on schedule like trains, and the board tells you in time. It refuses the category default of a split hero with a card mock-up beside copy.

OWN-WORLD: European concourse. Black ground, a charcoal board housing with split-flap tiles (warm white letters, amber for time and notices, signal red for anything due tomorrow or today). Station signal-yellow fields with black type, cool enamel white for information panels. One family, Archivo, with its width axis as the system: condensed caps on the board and signs, normal width for reading. Square-cornered signage controls, arrows and pictograms drawn as SVG.

STORY: The visitor sees their kind of life on a board, watches reminders arrive at 30, 7 and 1 day, tries adding something, believes it will catch things in time, and requests an invitation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full-width board is the hero. The headline flips in as the board's message rows, with "EXPIRED" in red. Below it are four sample rows (when, item, type, amount, remarks), blank rows, a live clock and the total due. Buttons on the board frame let the visitor add a preset; it flips into its slot, sorted by date. Under the board sit a short description and the email form with a yellow "Request an invitation" button.

FORM: Departure board, first of the seven grounded candidates. The roll ran after the user had already picked; the roll assigned candidate 7, but the user's pick stands. Signature interaction: split-flap cycling with re-sorting when an item is added. Motion grammar: flaps cycle in on load from left to right; nothing else on the page animates in its place.
